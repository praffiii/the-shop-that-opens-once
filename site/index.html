One shop. Seven nights. Seven keepsakes to return.

A cozy pixel-art mystery for macOS and Windows.

A village that lives its own day.

Everyone in Bellwood has somewhere to be. Talk to them and they stop, turn, and answer in their own voice, then go back to what they were doing. From morning to dusk the village keeps its own clock, and it remembers what you do for it.

Morning

The shop opens at midnight.

Once a night, for half an hour, a shop opens at the foot of the old banyan by the station. Marlow keeps the shop, and each night he gives Satria one keepsake that someone in Bellwood has lost.

  1. Midnight Take the keepsake from Marlow.
  2. Morning Ask around: who knew it?
  3. Midday Mend it with the one who can.
  4. Dusk Give it back, and a lantern goes out.

No quest markers, just people.

Nothing on the map tells you where to go. Turn each keepsake over and look closely: what you notice goes into your notes. Then show it around Bellwood, and listen for who remembers it.
