02 · Design pillars

Three rules we play by

No quest markers

No arrows, no checklist. You find your way by looking and listening.

Things connect people

Every keepsake leads to someone, and to the story they never finished.

Kindness over winning

No combat, no timer, no game over. A wrong guess just gets a reply.

03 · Core loop

One night, start to finish

Night 1 from the GDD, the old wristwatch, as a concept walkthrough.

Walk and look

Stroll Bellwood: the park, the bakery, the old station, the lane at dusk, the riverside.

Inspect

Turn a keepsake over. Read initials, unroll a film, check a number, listen closely.

Talk

Your questions open up people's memories. No answer can make you lose.

The 18:17 trigger

When a keepsake reaches its owner, the clock moves on and one lantern goes out.

Win

Return all seven keepsakes, then open the music box on the last night.

Fail

There isn't one. A wrong offer gets a puzzled reply or a new clue.

09 · Scope

Built one night at a time

From the team's feasibility report. Start small, prove it works, then grow.

1

Prove one night

Walking, talking, the bag, inspecting, one mystery with no markers, and the 18:17 clock.

2

MVP: two nights

Two playable chapters on PC. Playtest the big question: can players find each owner without help?

3

All seven nights

The full story and epilogue, about 1–3 hours. Mobile comes after the PC version is stable.

Players 16–35Who love story-rich indie games, cozy worlds and light mysteries.
Feels likeReflective, warm, bittersweet, gently curious.
